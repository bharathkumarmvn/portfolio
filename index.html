03 — Method

How a request becomes a system.

The same four steps every time. Most of the value is in the first one, which is why I sit inside the operation rather than next to it.

STEP 01

Understand

Sit with the people doing the work and find the decision behind the request. Half the asks I get are for a report that nobody needed once the real question surfaced.

STEP 02

Define

Agree the metric definitions with the stakeholders who will quote them, in writing, before any build. This is the step that stops two teams reporting different numbers for the same thing.

STEP 03

Build

Extract, model, validate, ship. Validation is part of the pipeline, not a later review, because a number that is quietly wrong costs more than a number that is late.

STEP 04

Prove

Measure the before and the after, hand it over with documentation, and train whoever inherits it. If it only works while I am watching, it is not finished.
